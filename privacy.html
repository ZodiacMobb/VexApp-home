VexApp privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · VexApp 1.0.0 by ZoNakama

The short version

VexApp does not collect, sell or share any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app works without an internet connection.

What stays on your phone

Your list, saved colors, room settings and app preferences are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. ZoNakama never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

If you match a color from a photo, the photo you pick or take is read on your device to find its colors. It is not uploaded or saved by VexApp. The app only asks for camera or photo access when you choose that feature, and you can say no.

Things the app does not use

Location, contacts, the microphone, advertising IDs and cross-app tracking.

Links

If you open a link to another website, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

VexApp is a general home-improvement helper. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends information off your device, this page will say exactly what, before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: vexapp.help@gmail.com
