VexApp help

VexApp 1.0.0 by ZoNakama

What it does

Measure a project and get the amounts to buy, build 8-color paint schemes, find and match colors across five paint brands, walk a 3D apartment or the outside of a house in your colors, compare sheens, practice sanding, staining and limewash, and read how-to guides.

Are the colors exact?

No screen shows paint exactly. Use VexApp to narrow it down, then look at a real chip or buy a sample before you paint a room.

Are the amounts exact?

They are estimates with waste already added. Coverage changes by product and surface, so check the label.

Where is my list saved?

Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it. Use Copy on My list to keep a copy somewhere else.

The 3D room is slow

Older phones drop the resolution automatically. Closing other apps helps.

Is VexApp connected to a store or a paint brand?

No. VexApp is independent. Brand names identify colors only.
